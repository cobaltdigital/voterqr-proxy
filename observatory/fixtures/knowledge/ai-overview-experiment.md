# AI overview citation experiment

Hypothesis: pages that answer a question in the first 60 words are cited in AI overviews more
often than pages that lead with brand context.

Method: pick 20 comparable service pages across clients, rewrite the opening of 10 of them to
lead with the direct answer, leave 10 unchanged, then track AI overview appearance rate weekly
for six weeks.

Guardrail: this is an experiment, not advice. Nothing here becomes a client recommendation until
the appearance rate difference clears noise on the watched signal.
