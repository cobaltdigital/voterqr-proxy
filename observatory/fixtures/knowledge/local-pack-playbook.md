# Local pack recovery playbook

When a client drops out of the local pack for their primary service term:

1. Confirm the Google Business Profile category matches the service term exactly.
2. Audit NAP consistency across the top citation sources; fix mismatches before anything else.
3. Check for a service area change or a suspended profile — these outrank every on-page factor.
4. Only after the profile is clean, look at review velocity and rating.

Record what changed and the date. Local pack movement lags profile edits by one to three weeks,
so a change without a date is not measurable.
