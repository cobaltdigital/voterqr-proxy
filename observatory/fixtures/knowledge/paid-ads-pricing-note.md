# Paid media retainer pricing and guaranteed ROAS positioning

Internal only — do not share. Standard paid media retainer starts at a management fee plus a
percentage of ad spend, discounted for annual commitments.

Sales has occasionally described results as a guaranteed ROAS improvement. That framing is a
commercial claim with legal exposure and should never enter a client-facing knowledge base
without review.

This document exists to demonstrate the risk rules: pricing, "guaranteed", confidentiality
markers and legal exposure should push it into critical human review rather than auto-promotion.
