What changed in local SEO this quarter

Local pack results are increasingly influenced by review velocity and by whether the Google Business Profile category exactly matches the searched service term. Citation consistency still matters, but its weight appears lower than it was two years ago.

Service area businesses

Service area businesses saw the largest movement in the local pack after category changes. Profiles that edited their primary category mid-quarter took roughly two to three weeks to settle.

What we expect next

Expect AI overviews to keep absorbing informational local queries, pushing organic clicks toward transactional service terms.
